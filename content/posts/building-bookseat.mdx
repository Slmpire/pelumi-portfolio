## Why BookSeat

Nigeria has a real ticketing problem. Most events still sell tickets via DMs and bank transfers.
BookSeat was my attempt at a proper solution — QR e-tickets, organiser dashboards, real payment flow.

## The stack decision

I went with React 18 and Firebase because the real-time database fit perfectly for live ticket inventory.
Paystack was the only real choice for Nigerian payments.

## The hardest part

QR code validation on the door. The scanner had to work offline, sync when back online, and prevent duplicate scans.
I solved it with a local validation cache and a background sync queue.

## What I'd do differently

Start with the organiser dashboard, not the buyer flow. The organiser is the real customer.
